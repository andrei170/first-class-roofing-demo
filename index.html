Flat roofing

EPDM done properly lasts decades. Done badly it lasts one winter.

Most flat roof failures are not the membrane. They are the falls, the upstands and the edge detail.

What we do

The parts nobody sees

  • Decking checked and replaced where it has gone soft
  • Laid to a proper fall so water actually leaves the roof
  • Upstands, trims and outlets detailed rather than bodged
  • One-piece EPDM where the span allows — fewer joints, fewer failures
